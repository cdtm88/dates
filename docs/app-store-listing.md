## Description (4,000 max)

Never miss a birthday, with enough warning to do something about it.

Dates keeps the birthdays, anniversaries and other yearly dates you care about in one simple list, in the order they're coming up. Its reminders arrive when they're useful: a week ahead when you need to buy a present, or a nudge on the morning itself when a message will do.

ONE LIST, IN THE ORDER THINGS HAPPEN
Upcoming dates come first, with how many days are left. Add the year and Dates shows the age or the number of years, and marks milestones like round birthdays and big anniversaries so you can plan for the ones that matter.

REMINDERS THAT FIT
Choose 7 days before, 3 days before, on the day, or any mix, for each date or for a whole group. Close family can get the full run of reminders while friends get a single nudge on the day, so your alerts stay worth reading.

ON YOUR HOME AND LOCK SCREEN
A widget shows the next few dates and their countdowns at a glance. On the lock screen, names stay hidden until you unlock.

BRING YOUR DATES WITH YOU
Import birthdays and anniversaries from your contacts, or any dates from a spreadsheet (CSV). A review screen shows what will be added and skips the ones you already have. Nothing is saved until you approve it. You can export to CSV at any time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Dates works offline. If you use iCloud, your dates sync privately across your devices through your own iCloud account. Otherwise they stay on your iPhone.

THOUGHTFUL DETAILS
• Leave out the year when you don't know it, and no age is shown
• 29 February birthdays fall on 28 February in other years
• Light and dark mode
• Made for iPhone, including the iPhone Duo

## App Review notes

No sign-in is needed. Every feature is available on first launch.

- To see content quickly, tap Add a date, or use Import from Contacts (optional, needs Contacts permission) on the empty screen.
- Reminders use local notifications. Permission is requested after the first date is saved.
- iCloud sync runs automatically when the device is signed in to iCloud. There's nothing to set up.
- The app collects no data (App Privacy: Data Not Collected).
