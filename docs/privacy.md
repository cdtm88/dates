# Dates privacy policy

**Effective 22 October 2026**

Dates is an iPhone app for remembering birthdays, anniversaries and other yearly dates. It is built to keep your information on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The developer does not collect it.

## The short version

- Dates collects no data. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.
- Your dates stay on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud storage.

## What Dates stores

Dates stores what you enter: names, dates, the type of date (birthday, anniversary and so on), groups and your reminder settings. It keeps them on your device. The developer cannot see them.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Dates syncs your dates to your other devices through your own **private iCloud database** (Apple's CloudKit). Apple stores this data under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your device.

## Contacts

Dates can import birthdays and anniversaries from your contacts, but only if you choose **Import from Contacts** and allow access.

- It reads only names, birthdays and other dates (such as anniversaries).
- Before anything is saved, it shows you a review screen, and only the entries you keep are saved.
- It never writes to, changes or uploads your contacts.

You can turn off contacts access at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts**.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device using the system's local notifications. They are not sent through any server.

## Widget

The home-screen widget shows your upcoming dates. To get them, it shares a storage area on the device with the app. On the lock screen, names are hidden while the device is locked.

## Import and export files

When you import a CSV file, Dates reads only the file you pick. When you export, it writes a file to the place you choose. In both cases the file goes where you send it, never to the developer.

## Deleting your data

- To delete a date, open it and tap **Delete date**. If you use iCloud, the deletion syncs to your other devices.
- To remove everything on a device, delete the app.
- To remove the iCloud copy, go to **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Dates** and delete the data.

## Children

Dates does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
